# Scrap art for Loot Scavenging

Hi! I'm making Loot Scavenging, a free RimWorld mod for 1.6 and 1.7, and I'm looking for someone to draw its scrap items.

In the mod, when a raider dies carrying gear your colony can't use yet, that gear turns into scrap, a pile of broken equipment from its era. You haul it to storage and later recycle it for materials or study it for research. There's one kind of scrap for each of six tech eras. Players will see a lot of these piles, in stockpiles and on battlefields, so each one should be easy to recognise at normal zoom.

## What I'm after

Six images, one per era, each showing a small pile of broken pieces. I'll make the bigger stack sizes myself out of your pieces, so please keep each piece on its own layer in the source file.

Here's roughly what I picture for each era. Treat the colours as a starting point. What matters most is that the six look different in shape and material too, not only in colour, so colourblind players can still tell them apart.

- **Neolithic.** Splintered bows and clubs, knapped stone blades, bone, bits of hide and cord. Warm wood and hide browns.
- **Medieval.** Bent and snapped blades, loose mail rings, dented plate, torn leather straps. Dull iron and leather.
- **Industrial.** Broken gun parts, springs, bolts, wire, cracked stocks, a shredded flak vest. Steel grey and gunmetal with a little olive.
- **Spacer.** Cracked plasteel plates, fused circuit boards, a dead power cell. Pale grey-white with muted cyan.
- **Ultratech.** Sleek fractured casings, shards of some exotic core, shapes you can't quite place. Dark slate with muted violet.
- **Archotech.** Dense, dark fragments that are far too precise, with fine gold tracery. Near-black with gold.

It should all look like junk. Nobody should mistake it for a working weapon or for a vanilla resource like steel, plasteel, components, wood or slag chunks.

## Style

These will sit right next to vanilla items, so I'd like them to match the base game:

- Top-down, like the vanilla resource stacks.
- A solid black outline with no gaps, about 4 to 6 px on a 128 px canvas (vanilla uses 2 to 3 px at 64 px per tile), and thinner black lines where two materials meet.
- A few big, smoothly shaded shapes rather than lots of small hard facets or white highlight streaks.
- Muted colours. Fully saturated colour glows against the ground.
- Nothing outside the outline, so no drop shadow, glow, rim light or second outline.
- Fixed colours. The game doesn't tint these.

The vanilla Steel, Components, Advanced components, Plasteel and Silver stacks, plus the stone and slag chunks, are good references.

## Files

Transparent PNGs at 128 × 128 px, one tile each, with the pile inside the tile and a little empty margin around it. Please name them `Scrap_Neolithic.png`, `Scrap_Medieval.png`, `Scrap_Industrial.png`, `Scrap_Spacer.png`, `Scrap_Ultratech.png` and `Scrap_Archotech.png`, and send the layered source files as well.
