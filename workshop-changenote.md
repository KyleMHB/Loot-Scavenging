Ammunition beyond your colony's reach now drops whole instead of turning into scrap. A new setting, "Drop mode includes ammunition", brings back the old behaviour. When ammunition is scrapped, a stack now gives about what it's worth instead of one scrap per round.

The Cleanup preset now deletes all ammunition from the hostile dead.

"Everything stays on the body" now covers downed pawns too: they keep their weapon in hand and their inventory in their pack instead of dropping them.
